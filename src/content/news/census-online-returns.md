<p class="lead">The hardest people to count are the ones missing from online records. The 2021 Census gives us a rare way to see them.</p>

<div class="bp-stat"><b>63.8%</b><span>of Hull’s Census 2021 returns were made online, against 88.9% across England and Wales</span></div>

## Paper first

The Office for National Statistics expected many Hull households to struggle online, so it sent **58.9%** of them a paper form first. Across England the figure was just 9%.

## Why it matters

More and more services are online first: benefits, GP appointments, council tax, school admissions and job applications. Every household that can’t get online risks missing out, paying more, or waiting longer.

> Being invisible in the data can’t be mapped ward by ward, because the people affected are the ones missing from the records.

## What we’re doing

We work through libraries, community hubs and local groups, where people already go, so help reaches residents who won’t find it online.
