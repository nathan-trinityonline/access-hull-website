<p class="lead">Thousands of working laptops and phones sit in drawers. Donated and refurbished, they can help children with homework and adults find work.</p>

<div class="bp-stat"><b>14%</b><span>of Hull pupils aged 8 to 16 had no device at home in 2021</span></div>

## The checklist

1. **Back up** any photos and files you want to keep.
2. **Sign out** of your Apple ID or Google account, and turn off Find My iPhone.
3. **Remove** your SIM card and any memory card.
4. **Factory reset** it if you can. Don’t worry if not: we wipe every device anyway.
5. **Find the charger** and include it if you still have it.

## What happens next

We securely wipe every device, refurbish what we can and give it to residents, schools and job seekers through our hubs. Anything we can’t reuse is recycled responsibly.

## Donating as a business?

We collect end-of-life IT in bulk and provide a data-wipe certificate for every device.
