<p class="lead">Hull has some of the best broadband in the country. Almost every home can get full fibre. But a fast connection outside the door doesn’t mean people are online inside it.</p>

## A city near the top for risk

Hull ranks **36th of 314** English councils for digital exclusion risk. **39%** of Hull’s neighbourhoods fall in England’s riskiest 10%.

<div class="bp-stat"><b>39%</b><span>of Hull neighbourhoods are in England’s riskiest 10% for digital exclusion</span></div>

## The same three wards come out on top

Orchard Park, St Andrew’s &amp; Docklands and Marfleet are the top three wards on both deprivation and digital exclusion risk. Across all 21 wards the two rankings line up closely.

## Age matters as much as income

Pickering, Sutton and Ings rise seven or eight places when you look at digital risk rather than deprivation. More than a fifth of their residents are 65 or over, against about 11% in Central and North Carr.

> Help for older people needs to reach beyond the most deprived estates.

## Ward averages hide hotspots

North Carr sits 13th overall, yet **7** of its neighbourhoods are in England’s riskiest 10%. One Derringham neighbourhood is in the riskiest 1%.

## Connection isn’t the problem

Hull’s broadband score is better than the England average. The risk comes from cost, age, health and skills, which is why Access:Hull focuses on affordable access, devices and confidence.
