<p class="lead">Social tariffs are cheaper broadband and mobile plans for people on benefits. They work just like normal plans, with no long contracts, but cost much less.</p>

<div class="bp-stat"><b>70%</b><span>of eligible households didn’t know social tariffs exist (October 2025)</span></div>

## Who can get one?

Most social tariffs are for people who get Universal Credit, Pension Credit, Jobseeker’s Allowance, Employment and Support Allowance or Income Support. Some also accept PIP and other disability benefits.

Both run month to month. Apply online at kcom.com/flex or call KCOM on 01482 602555.

## Mobile phones

- **O2 Essential Plan:** £10 a month, 10GB data
- **SMARTY Social Tariff (Three network):** £12 a month, unlimited data
- **VOXI For Now (Vodafone network):** £10 a month, unlimited data for six months

## Before you switch

1. Have your National Insurance number or a benefit letter ready.
2. Ask your current provider first. You may be able to move without changing your router or SIM.
3. Compare with normal deals. Ordinary SIM-only plans are sometimes cheaper.
