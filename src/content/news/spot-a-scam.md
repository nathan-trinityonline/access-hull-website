<p class="lead">Scams are one of the main reasons people give up on going online. Knowing the warning signs helps you stay safe and confident.</p>

## Warning signs

- It’s urgent: "your account will be closed today"
- It asks for a PIN, password or a code sent to your phone
- It asks you to pay by bank transfer or gift card
- The link or email address looks slightly wrong

> Your bank will never ask you to move money to a "safe account".

## What to do

1. **Stop.** Don’t click links or call numbers in the message.
2. **Check.** Contact the organisation using a number you trust, like the one on your bank card.
3. **Report it.** Forward scam texts to **7726** and scam emails to **report@phishing.gov.uk**.

If you think a call is from a scammer pretending to be your bank, hang up and call **159** to reach your bank safely.

## Look out for each other

Older people living alone are often targeted. In Hull this is most common in Pickering, Ings, Bricknell and Derringham. A quick chat with a relative or neighbour can stop a scam.

<div class="bp-take"><h3>Remember</h3><p>If something feels wrong, take your time. A real organisation won’t mind you checking.</p></div>
