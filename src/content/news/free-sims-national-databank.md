<p class="lead">The National Databank is like a food bank for mobile data. Phone networks donate free SIMs and data, and local groups hand them out to people who need them.</p>

## What you get

- **O2:** 25GB of data plus UK calls and texts for 30 days
- **VodafoneThree:** 40GB of data plus calls and texts for 30 days, renewing for six months

You can get up to 12 months in total, through one SIM or monthly top-up vouchers.

<div class="bp-stat"><b>12 months</b><span>of free mobile data, calls and texts, for adults on a low income</span></div>

## Who can get one?

Adults aged 18 and over on a low income who don’t have internet at home or on the go, or can’t afford their contract or top-ups. You don’t need to be on benefits.

## Where to go in Hull

West Hull Community Hub at Johnny Whiteley Park, North Road, gives out free SIMs on Thursdays (10am to 3pm) and Fridays (10am to 12:30pm). Other hubs can refer you.
